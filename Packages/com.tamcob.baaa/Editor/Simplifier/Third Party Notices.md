# Third Party Notices

## Meshia Mesh Simplification

The mesh simplifier in this folder comes from Meshia Mesh Simplification by Ram.Type-0
(https://github.com/RamType0/Meshia.MeshSimplification), taken from the touma-tw fork
"Mesh Simplification - Touma Fork" (https://github.com/touma-tw/Meshia.MeshSimplification).
Both are released under the MIT License. The license text and copyright holder are in LICENSE.md.

We changed it with a new namespace, editor-side mesh reads, bug fixes and new merge rules (locked vertices, bone overlap, blendshape stretch, error curve, seam and border aware collapses).
